*** Believes

* Wins and losses are equally a part of trading. That metaphor is the real secret of the Holy Grail.
* Stock price is moving by institutions.
* Institutions buying will leave traces on volume.
* Berakout without volume has high chance of failure.
* Don’t obsess over setups — obsess over finding low-risk entry points in strong names, and improving your risk-to-reward ratio.



** Hard Rules

* Do not enter a trade if Risk of LoD > 60% ATR.
* Skip trades when the extension in ADR < 25%. Measure extension in ADR before buy, not to find a ceiling but to stop myself buying too early: under a quarter ADR off the low the stop sits inside the noise, and 91 percent of those trades died on it.
